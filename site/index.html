Map guide

Explore the collection

Open Birds to choose collection layers. Select a marker to read its catalogue record. A ring around a marker means several records share that position. Choose a catalogue number to open one.

Read a location

Egg records can use an approximate locality, county, or state/province position. The record names the method and preserves the catalogue’s original locality wording.

Keyboard navigation

Focus the map and use the arrow keys to pan. Use + and − to zoom. Press Enter to open the visible location nearest the map center. Press Escape to close a record.
