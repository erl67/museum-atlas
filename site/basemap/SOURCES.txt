Museum Atlas basemap

Natural Earth provides public-domain geographic data. The bundled geometry
comes from the v5.1.2 repository tag. All geography is in WGS 84 longitude
and latitude.

https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2/geojson
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

world.geojson combines these 1:110m datasets.
  ne_110m_admin_0_countries
  ne_110m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land
  ne_110m_coastline
  ne_110m_lakes
  ne_110m_rivers_lake_centerlines

provinces.geojson uses ne_50m_admin_1_states_provinces_lines. Coordinates
were rounded to three decimal places and the regional boundary lines were
simplified with a 0.02-degree tolerance. This 1:50m Natural Earth layer
covers Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, India, Indonesia, Russia, South
Africa and the United States.

labels.geojson includes Natural Earth's country label positions and the
regional label points from ne_50m_admin_1_states_provinces. The atlas adds
its own continent, ocean and sea label positions. The style draws its
latitude and longitude grid at runtime. The geographic data and fonts ship
with the atlas, including the geometry used when visitors zoom in.
